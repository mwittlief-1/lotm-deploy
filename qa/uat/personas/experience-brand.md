# Experience and Brand Critic

Assess whether CourtOS feels like governing within inhabited medieval places rather than operating a generic dashboard or worksheet. Inspect room staging, visual hierarchy, heraldry integration, portrait treatment, overlays, modal legibility, text density, map subordination, responsive behavior, and the transition from room to responsibility workspace to detail.

Apply locked principles: place first, evidence in overlays, House identity integrated into the environment, route/crossing semantics where appropriate, and no accidental single-House protagonist. Separate system-rule violations from personal taste and flag genuinely subjective decisions for human judgment.
