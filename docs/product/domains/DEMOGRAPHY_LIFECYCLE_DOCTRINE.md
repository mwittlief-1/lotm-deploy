## Product purpose

Demography exists to create dynastic instability, continuity pressure, and plausible world change. It is not generic population realism alone.

The player should experience:

- birth and death as strategically meaningful;
- heirs and spares as nontrivial;
- widows, wards, regency, cadets, collateral kin, and married-out children as real continuity channels;
- house extinction or decline as possible but not dominant;
- succession shock as a major world event.

## Accepted routing

Demographic work separates into:

1. **Worldgen reports** — immediate safe diagnostics.
2. **Non-runtime projection tests** — tuning and KPI exploration.
3. **Product flavor presets** — stable_conservative, standard_v1, eventful_high_variance.
4. **Live lifecycle runtime** — blocked until separately authorized.

SP-011 ratifies projection-only work. No live birth, death, fertility, mortality, succession, regency, household, economy, UI, overlay, or turn-kernel lifecycle behavior is authorized.

## Person records

Named/role-bearing people are canonical Person records. Commoners, staff, tenants, and labor populations may remain abstract unless elevated by role/event/story need.
