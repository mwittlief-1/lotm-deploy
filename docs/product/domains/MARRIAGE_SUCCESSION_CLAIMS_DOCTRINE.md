## Core doctrine

The player manages a house across time, not only a manor economy. Marriage, succession, heirs, cadets, widows, wards, claims, and regency are central to dynastic play.

## Marriage

v0.3 cannot rely only on passive Prospects. It needs constrained outbound marriage agency. Prospects, inbound offers, and outbound pursuit should share an underlying Marriage Opportunity concept.

Dowry cannot remain flavor. Marriage must touch economy/fiscal consequence.

v0.3 marriage payments use one-time settlement only; installments, land-backed dowries, dower, jointure, and debt instruments are deferred.

## REC-053 v0.3 rule

A/R/T relationship terms classify proposals as:

- clean;
- strained;
- blocked_by_relationship_terms.

A/R/T does **not** hide candidates, reorder candidates, or change dowry in v0.3.

SP-003 ratifies REC-053 as a narrow relationship-terms classifier only. Required negative cases for future proof planning: hidden dowry debt is rejected, arrears/installments are rejected, and A/R/T-modified dowry is rejected.

## Succession

Player succession is mandatory for v0.3. Head death must resolve or surface a continuation/failure state.

Dead active heads/officers cannot remain active actors. Visible T1 house heads, king, liege, stewards, and key officers must resolve to successor, vacancy, acting administrator, or explicit unresolved state.

Minor child may remain legal heir/head; widow/steward/regent/guardian/caretaker may be shown as acting administrator.

Married-out children remain visible in dynasty/family surfaces but leave household/residence/provisioning surfaces unless exception applies.

## Stop rules

Codex must not implement generalized succession, live regency/wardship, marriage market behavior, claims enforcement, dowry debt, or A/R/T-modified dowry without explicit approval.
