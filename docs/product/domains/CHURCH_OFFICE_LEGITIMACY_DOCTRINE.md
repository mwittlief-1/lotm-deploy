## Core doctrine

The Church is not flavor. It affects legitimacy, education, recordkeeping, family placement, political access, marriage law, dispute mediation, patronage, and memory.

## Actor distinction

Bishopric institution, bishop office, bishop person, and bishop birth house are distinct.

Named clergy are canonical Persons with birth-house/provenance and lineage. If bishoprics exist, bishops are canonical known institution holders.

Church kin are not player puppets. They may favor the house, but retain institutional duties.

SP-014 ratifies the office/church/legitimacy boundary contract. It explicitly rejects global piety score direction and church-family puppet behavior as implementation shortcuts.

## v1 church structure

Strong-draft base-v1 structure:

- local parish;
- abbey/convent/monastic house;
- bishopric;
- abstract higher church;
- rare bounded papal legate/event actor.
