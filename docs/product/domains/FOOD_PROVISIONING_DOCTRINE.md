## Product purpose

Food/provisioning exists to make household survival, estate resilience, social obligation, and local hardship legible.

The player must be able to answer:

- Does the manor have enough food?
- Why did stores rise or fall?
- Who consumed or claimed food?
- What shortage or surplus means strategically?
- What can be done to recover?

## Core principles

- Food is not Coin.
- 1 Food Unit = 30 bushels.
- 1 Coin = 30 bushels is a planning/accounting equivalence only and does not collapse Food into Coin.
- Food changes require receipts.
- Shortage must matter but remain bounded/recoverable in v0.3.
- Meat/hunting is supplemental in v0.3 and must not replace the staple food loop.
- Tithe should be goods/Food-first and clearly explained unless commuted.
- The approved tithe planning anchor is 2 Coin-equivalent/year, approximately 60 bushels/year under the approved Food Unit scale.
- The current 5 percent production tithe preview is runtime evidence, not product canon.

## SP-002 ratified planning boundary

Food demand, store, and shortage examples may be used for docs/proof planning only. They must be explicitly non-mutating and must not create live Food deltas, live store carryover, hidden market conversion, or obligation settlement behavior.
