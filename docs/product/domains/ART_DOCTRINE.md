## Definitions

**Allegiance** is willingness to remain aligned, loyal, cooperative, or obligated under pressure.

**Respect** is perceived legitimacy, competence, honor, dignity, seriousness, and status-performance.

**Threat** is perceived danger, coercive capacity, fear, suspicion, and ability/willingness to impose cost.

## Design value

A/R/T distinguishes relationships that a single friendship score cannot:

- loyal but unimpressed;
- respected rival;
- feared tyrant;
- beloved weak lord;
- holy bishop;
- resentful but compliant tenant.

## Edges

A/R/T may eventually apply to:

- person-person;
- house-house;
- head-spouse;
- head-children;
- spouse-children;
- head-officer;
- liege-vassal;
- church-house;
- local community-house;
- claimant-holder;
- tenant-lord.

## Receipts and memory markers

Material relationship changes should be traceable through receipts or memory markers: who remembers, what happened, confidence, moral frame, decay, future use.

## SP-007 planning frame

Hidden/banded A/R/T direction is ratified for taxonomy, edge planning, memory-marker planning, and action-delta matrix drafting. Final deltas, decay, caps, initialization rules, and live consumers are not ratified.

## Stop rules

Codex may preserve A/R/T hooks and preview structures, but may not initialize live relationship graph, mutate values, infer final delta magnitudes as canon, or use A/R/T to alter live outcomes without authorization.
