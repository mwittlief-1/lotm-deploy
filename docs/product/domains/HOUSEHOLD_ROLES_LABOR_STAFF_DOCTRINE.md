## Core doctrine

The player should feel like the head of a medieval household ruling through people, not an omnipotent manager manipulating abstract resources.

## v0.3 proof scope

v0.3 should prove delegated household governance, not complete household simulation.

Accepted elements:

- major officers exist and matter;
- visible vacancies create modest, legible consequences;
- player can appoint or replace at least one household office;
- ordinary staff are grouped into capacity bands, not individual simulation;
- tenant/labor household availability should not collapse into generic population;
- labor availability affects at least one concrete result;
- role/staff/labor effects must be receipt-backed.

## Role distinction

Separate:

- formal office;
- household role;
- current assignment;
- capacity load;
- reporting path;
- competence/suitability;
- authority basis.

## Stop rules

Codex must not create full household simulation as a side effect of v0.3 proof work.
