## Definition

Standing coverage is the recurring work required to keep a house, manor, household, estate, and social position functioning. It exists whether or not the player chooses a discrete action.

## Accepted coverage categories

- Household operations;
- Manor daily operations;
- Fiscal administration;
- Estate condition / maintenance;
- Provisioning / stores;
- Local order / grievance sensing;
- Records / knowledge custody;
- Court / liege watch;
- Church / parish / bishopric watch;
- Military / security readiness;
- Marriage / dynasty watch;
- Portfolio oversight.

## Design rules

- Standing coverage may consume all or part of an actor’s capacity.
- Poor coverage should degrade outcomes or increase risk.
- Strong coverage should improve detection, reliability, and ordinary resilience, not eliminate bad luck.
- Standing directives may automate routine handling, but they still consume capacity and create receipts or consequences when material.
- Coverage should scale with manor count, household size, rank, crisis state, geography, and obligation complexity.
