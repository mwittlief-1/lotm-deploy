## Core doctrine

Place matters. The player starts in a specific manor within a specific local, feudal, ecclesiastical, and geographic context.

## v0.3 map scope

v0.3 should not attempt full strategic map gameplay, full mapgen rewrite, route simulation, or mature realm geography.

v0.3 proof target: the player starts with a clearly described home manor whose terrain, access, neighbors, and local context produce visible consequences.

Minimum home-manor identity:

- name;
- broad terrain profile;
- lordship/county context;
- nearest church/market/liege connection;
- small local condition set.

Minimum local world surface:

- neighboring manor/house;
- liege seat;
- local church;
- market/town or equivalent;
- environmental feature.

Current medium realm frame:

Older 25-county / ~2,000-manor / ~2,500-village / 200–300-abbey assumptions are retired for the current lane.

## Holding fabric principles

- Hexes are productive-capacity units.
- Settlements are nodes.
- Manors are catchments.
- Lordship is assigned over manor/holding units.
- Counties/bishoprics are overlays.
- `county_domain` means count’s direct demesne plus county-seat office-support land, not blanket county ownership.
- `church_fief` remains one top-level type in v0.1 with holder metadata.
- Market/fair/toll rights are franchise rights, not automatic settlement-class consequences.
