## Doctrine

Military readiness/service is a standing capacity and obligation domain, not merely an event response.

It should include:

- arms/equipment upkeep;
- household retainers;
- training/readiness;
- muster and service obligation;
- personal service vs contingent vs payment/substitute vs refusal;
- local security readiness;
- fortification as exceptional preparation;
- war/liege summons as cutpoint pressure.

SP-015 ratifies readiness/service boundary planning only. It does not authorize tactical warfare, live service execution, service obligation mutation, or turn-pipeline integration.

## Action-family mapping

Military processes map to normal action families:

- maintain arms → Build / Repair / Maintain / Fortify;
- inspect readiness → Investigate / Verify / Monitor;
- muster men → Summon / Serve / Refuse;
- hire retainer → Offer / Bargain / Bind;
- escort → Send / Receive / Recall Person.

## Stop rules

This doctrine does not authorize tactical warfare, generalized civil war systems, or live service obligations without approval.
