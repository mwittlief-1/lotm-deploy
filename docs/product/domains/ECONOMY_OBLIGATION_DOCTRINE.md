## Resource spine

The economy spine includes:

- Coin;
- Food;
- Labor Pressure;
- Condition;
- Order;
- Obligations.

These should not collapse into one currency.

## Coin

Coin is liquid spendable estate wealth. It is not total wealth. A powerful landed house can be land-rich and Coin-poor.

## Food

Food remains distinct from Coin because subsistence, storage, famine, provisioning, harvest risk, household survival, and social obligation must be legible.

## Labor Pressure

Labor pressure is a manpower/capacity/strain constraint, not stored money.

## Actor fiscal state

Major actors should not spend from nowhere. Significant houses, institutions, and offices need fiscal state, bounded fiscal bands, or explicitly modeled pools.

## Income/obligation row integrity

Manor income and obligations should preserve:

- source;
- counterparty;
- form;
- authority basis;
- recurrence;
- liquidity;
- relationship/order implications;
- receipt/provenance.

SP-013 ratifies this obligation catalog frame for docs/catalog planning only. It does not authorize live obligations, dues, arrears, settlement, Coin/Food mutation, or resource mutation.

## v0.3 accepted constraints

- Standard fixture starting treasury: 20 Coin within one-manor band 18-22 Coin.
- Do not retune starting treasury until ordinary income, expenses, obligations, and receipts are wired truthfully.
- v0.3 dowry affordability uses hard funding gate; no hidden dowry debt, arrears, installments, or non-cash settlement.
- 20 Coin/year is gross support capacity, not net income.

## Approved v0.3 numeric planning anchors

These anchors are canon for planning, specs, catalogs, requirements, and proof design. They do not authorize runtime mutation.

- 1 Coin = 30 bushels as planning/accounting equivalence only, not universal market price.
- 1 Food Unit = 30 bushels.
- Food remains distinct from Coin.
- Standard manor gross support: 20 Coin/year.
- Standard manor gross support planning band: 18-24 Coin/year.
- Tax: 1 Coin/year; routine band 0.5-2 Coin/year.
- Tithe: goods-first, 2 Coin-equivalent/year, approximately 60 bushels/year.
- Scutage: 4 Coin event anchor; ordinary band 2-6 Coin.
- Relief: 10 Coin event anchor; ordinary band 6-12 Coin.
- Household service: 3 Coin/year.
- Grouped staff: 3-6 Coin/year.
- Improvement/franchise upkeep: 10-30 percent, working anchor 20 percent; this is improvement/franchise-specific upkeep planning guidance, not an automatic whole-manor annual haircut.
- Dowry: standard 30 Coin; ordinary band 20-50 Coin; hard funding gate mandatory.
- Event pressure bands by annual gross support capacity: routine 0-5 percent, local/serious 5-15 percent, serious 15-30 percent, crisis 30-50 percent.
