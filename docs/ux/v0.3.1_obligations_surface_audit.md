# LoM UX Audit — v0.3.1 Obligations, Arrears, and Counterparty Surfaces

**Task:** `V03-R1-005-T01`  
**Scope:** inventory the current gameplay-screen surfaces that expose obligations, arrears, counterparty identity, gifts, offerings, and receipts; identify where the current shell duplicates or blurs those facts; and leave a task-ready handoff for the follow-on UI contract work.  
**Constraint:** audit only. No mechanic changes and no new UI contract in this task.

---

## 1. Current gameplay homes

The current gameplay shell exposes obligations and related fiscal pressure across these surfaces:

| Surface | Current home | What it shows now | Counterparty clarity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agenda | `src/ui/playScreenModel.ts` | one aggregate `Review obligations` agenda item when any dues are present | None. No liege/church split. |
| Turn report | `src/ui/panels/TurnReportPanel.tsx` | total obligations, due entering, accrued, arrears | None. All values are aggregated as `coin / bushels`. |
| Decisions | `src/ui/panels/DecisionsPanel.tsx` | pay coin, pay bushels, shortfall to arrears, war levy choice | None for church vs liege. Inputs are split by asset, not counterparty. |
| Diff ledger | `src/ui/playScreenModel.ts` | relationship drift summary and generic obligations helper copy | Partial. Liege pressure can be implied; church pressure is not distinctly surfaced. |
| Receipts explainer | `src/ui/playScreenReceipts.ts` and `src/ui/panels/ReceiptsViewerPanel.tsx` | grouped/raw receipt lines behind food, coin, unrest, and overview focuses | Weak. Counterparties appear only inside receipt prose, not as explicit UI grouping. |
| Court budget | `src/ui/playScreenCourtBudget.ts` and `src/ui/panels/DecisionsPanel.tsx` | `Gift to liege` and `Offering to church` budget costs | Strong labels here, but disconnected from obligation and receipt surfaces. |
| Copy layer | `src/App.tsx` | generic obligations helper and labels | Aggregate-only language. No named counterparties. |

The current shell therefore has enough raw information to explain obligations, but it does not yet give liege and church pressure one stable, player-facing home.

## 2. Fact inventory by surface

### Turn report

`TurnReportPanel` is the current resolved-turn accounting home for obligations:

- `Total obligations`
- `Due entering turn`
- `Accrued this turn`
- `Arrears (carried forward)`
- a generic helper: unpaid obligations become arrears and can raise unrest and relationship risk

What is missing:

- no explicit liege row
- no explicit church row
- no indication which asset maps to which counterparty unless the player already remembers the rules
- no visible enforcement-stage summary

### Decisions

`DecisionsPanel` is the current planning home:

- `Pay coin`
- `Pay bushels`
- `Shortfall → arrears`
- optional war levy selector

What is missing:

- the player chooses payment by asset only, not by named counterparty
- there is no explicit explanation that `coin` means liege and `bushels` means church
- the same aggregate numbers from Turn Report are repeated again here, so the player sees the totals twice but still not the counterparties

### Diff ledger and agenda

The shell already treats obligations as strategically important:

- `buildCouncilAgendaItems()` raises `Review obligations` when dues are present
- `buildDiffLedgerItems()` may attach the generic obligations helper when the liege relationship moved while arrears already existed

What is missing:

- the agenda card does not name the counterparty driving the urgency
- the diff ledger only gets specific when liege relationship movement is inferred
- church enforcement can become visible only as a generic stability or relationship consequence, not as its own fiscal headline

### Receipts explainer

The receipts modal is the richest evidence surface today, but it is grouped by resource focus:

- `overview`
- `food`
- `coin`
- `unrest`

That helps explain what changed, but it does not help the player answer:

- what do I owe the liege right now?
- what do I owe the church right now?
- which receipt lines belong to obligations vs gifts vs offerings for the same counterparty?

Counterparty identity currently survives only in receipt text, not in the viewer structure.

### Gifts and offerings

`playScreenCourtBudget.ts` already gives clear labels for:

- `Gift to liege`
- `Offering to church`

This is the strongest current counterparty naming in the gameplay shell. The issue is that it lives in the court budget surface, not in the obligations/receipts explanation path. As a result:

- the player can see the counterparty name when reading budget costs
- but the same names are not preserved as first-class groups in the receipts explainer
- and there is no shared vocabulary tying gifts, offerings, dues, arrears, and enforcement together

## 3. Duplicate and ambiguous displays

### Duplicate cluster A: obligations totals without counterparties

The shell repeats aggregate obligations in both:

- `TurnReportPanel`
- `DecisionsPanel`

This mirrors the older v0.3.0 timing split correctly, but the player still has to mentally translate:

- coin = liege
- bushels = church

So the shell currently duplicates the totals before it clarifies the ownership.

### Duplicate cluster B: obligation pressure appears in multiple explanation systems

Obligation pressure currently appears through:

- agenda severity
- diff ledger relationship drift
- grouped/raw receipts
- generic helper copy in both report and decisions

These surfaces do not use one shared counterparty vocabulary. The player can read all of them and still not know whether the active pressure is:

- liege tax
- church tithe
- both

### Ambiguity cluster C: receipts are grouped by resource, not by counterparty

Current receipt grouping makes sense for resource chips, but it blurs fiscal ownership:

- church dues naturally land in food-focused receipts
- liege dues naturally land in coin-focused receipts
- arrears and enforcement pressure can also land in unrest-focused receipts

That means one fiscal story can be split across three receipt focuses, with no counterparty sub-group to hold it together.

### Ambiguity cluster D: gifts and offerings are named clearly, but only in budget language

`Gift to liege` and `Offering to church` are already explicit. The ambiguity is that the player sees those labels:

- in the court budget box
- but not as matching top-level groups in the obligations/receipts explanation stack

So gifts and offerings already have better counterparty naming than the dues surface they should eventually sit beside.

### Ambiguity cluster E: enforcement identity is implied rather than shown

The gameplay shell can currently imply enforcement through:

- higher unrest
- relation drift
- receipt lines mentioning arrears or carry

It does not currently show:

- stage-one enforcement status by counterparty
- whether a counterparty is clear versus in arrears
- whether the shell is showing settled, carried, or projected pressure

## 4. What changed technically in R1-003-T06, and what the UI still does not use

`V03-R1-003-T06` added `economy_obligations_view` to bounded snapshots through the experience-domain seam. That gives the UI lane a canonical next-step data source for:

- liege-first counterparty ordering
- per-counterparty due and arrears amounts
- settlement flags
- stage-one enforcement state and summaries

The current gameplay shell does not yet consume that view. So the core mismatch is now:

- data contract exists in snapshot/view-model space
- gameplay UI still renders aggregate asset totals and resource-grouped receipts

This is exactly the handoff point for `V03-R1-005-T02` and `V03-R1-005-T03`.

## 5. Gifts and offerings presentation needs

Current needs for the follow-on task set are now clear:

1. Gifts and offerings should share a counterparty grouping language with obligations.
   - `liege`: tax due, arrears, gift, enforcement
   - `church`: tithe due, arrears, offering, enforcement

2. Receipt grouping needs a counterparty-first explanation path.
   - resource focus can remain useful
   - but the shell also needs a way to keep one counterparty’s receipts together

3. The shell needs one explicit place where the player can read:
   - what is due now
   - what is already in arrears
   - what happens if it stays unpaid

4. Gifts and offerings should not be presented as a separate unnamed “court” concept once the player opens deeper explanation.
   - the budget box can stay as a fast summary
   - the explainer surface should reconnect those actions to liege/church identity

---

## 6. Task-ready conclusions

1. The gameplay shell already exposes obligations repeatedly, but it exposes counterparties weakly.
2. The biggest current UX gap is not missing numbers; it is the lack of one stable liege/church grouping across report, decisions, ledger, and receipts.
3. `TurnReportPanel` and `DecisionsPanel` should remain separate resolved-versus-planning homes, but they need the same counterparty vocabulary instead of only aggregate asset totals.
4. The receipts explainer should stop relying on keyword-only resource grouping as the sole structure for obligations, gifts, and offerings.
5. `economy_obligations_view` is the correct canonical seam for the next UI task. The follow-on work should consume that view rather than inventing a second obligations contract in `src/ui`.

---

## 7. Recommended follow-on direction

For `V03-R1-005-T02`:

- define one deterministic counterparty contract around:
  - liege obligations
  - church obligations
  - liege gifts
  - church offerings
- keep it presentation-only and aligned with receipt categories

For `V03-R1-005-T03`:

- render one visible obligations explainer surface that:
  - names liege and church directly
  - keeps resolved-state facts separate from planning controls
  - lets receipts remain drill-down evidence rather than the first place a player learns who is owed what

This audit should be treated as the canonical inventory for the obligations/counterparty UI pass.
