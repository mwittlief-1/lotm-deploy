## 2. Current counterparty presentation contract

The gameplay shell should now be read with these rules:

1. `TurnReportPanel` is the resolved-state obligations home.
   - It shows what is due now, what is overdue, whether arrears carried, which stage is active, and which relationship lever belongs to that counterparty.

2. `DecisionsPanel` is the planning home.
   - It keeps the same liege/church vocabulary, but its helper copy is framed as next-turn response rather than replaying the same facts in a second voice.

3. `ObligationsDetailPanel` is the counterparty explainer.
   - It keeps three concepts together for one named counterparty:
   - due and arrears
   - enforcement stage and this-turn outcome
   - next-turn response plus relationship lever

4. `ReceiptsViewerPanel` is now dual-structured in grouped mode.
   - top = counterparty-first explainer cards
   - below = resource-grouped sections and raw receipts

5. Gifts and offerings are not a separate unnamed court concept once a player opens deeper explanation.
   - `liege` owns tax, arrears, pressure, and gift
   - `church` owns tithe, arrears, pressure, and offering

This is the canonical v0.3.1 UI contract unless a later backlog task explicitly reopens the obligations surface design.

---

## 3. Stable UI rules for follow-on work

These rules should stay intact for content, portfolio, and follow-on implementation:

- Keep one counterparty vocabulary across report, decisions, detail sheet, and receipts.
- Preserve the resolved-versus-next-turn timing split.
- Keep stage labels player-facing and bounded. Do not expose rule ids, registry keys, or hidden enforcement internals in primary UI.
- Keep gifts and offerings attached to liege/church identity, even when they originate from court budget state.
- Let receipts stay evidence and drill-down support, not the first place a player has to infer who is owed what.

---

## 4. Deferred after v0.3.1

The following still remain outside the shipped v0.3.1 UI pass:

- agenda and diff-ledger copy still summarize obligation urgency at a higher level than the dedicated counterparty surfaces
- payment controls are still asset-first inputs rather than per-counterparty action rows
- no new sim-owned obligations contract was added beyond consuming the existing bounded `economy_obligations_view`
- no new church institution, clergy-placement, or broader political-pressure UI was introduced here

These should stay deferred unless a later backlog task explicitly reopens them.

---

## 5. Handoff for content and portfolio work

Content and portfolio follow-on work should treat the current obligations shell as stable enough to reference directly:

- content can now write liege- and church-specific helper copy against one shared UI contract
- portfolio planning can assume the obligations legibility epic has one stable explainer surface, one stable receipts path, and one stable resolved-versus-planning split
- later work should extend these surfaces rather than re-fragmenting facts into separate budget-only, receipt-only, or asset-only explanations

Suggested review question for future playtests:

> Can a player explain who is owed what, which pressure stage is already active, and which lever they can still use next turn without translating coin-versus-bushels rules in their head?
