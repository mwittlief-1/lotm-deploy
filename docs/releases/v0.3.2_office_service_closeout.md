## Scope

This closeout records the accepted `v0.3.2` office and service baseline
after `T01` through `T05`. It supersedes the opening audit as the current
release-facing summary of what office and service seams now guarantee,
which invariants are intentionally locked, and what later agenda, family,
and clergy work may safely assume.

## Locked `v0.3.2` office and service rules

### House and realm seat baselines are explicit

The accepted baseline now assumes:

- player-house seats remain `steward`, `clerk`, and `marshal`
- `steward` is the only required player-house seat
- required seats keep a one-turn vacancy cap
- realm seats remain `chancellor`, `chamberlain`, and `constable`
- both house and realm seat order is canonical and deterministic

### Service records preserve tenure unless placement actually changes

The accepted service baseline now assumes:

- unchanged holder mirrors do not rotate active service records
- a new service record opens only when holder or placement metadata changes
- successor and predecessor record links are explicit and deterministic
- active record ordering remains stable by canonical record id

### Placement targets are explicit

Court service records now carry placement continuity fields directly:

- `serve_at_actor_id`
- optional `institution_assignment_id`

That means later work can distinguish:

- ordinary court service attached to a serving actor
- institution-backed service such as a parish-facing placement

without inventing a parallel placement table inside agenda or clergy work.

### Retainer staffing and succession hooks are bounded but real

The accepted baseline now includes:

- explicit `retainer_upkeep` versus `family_service` payment bases
- deterministic classification of household members versus non-family
  retainers for player-house seats
- owner-succession hook summaries keyed off the claimant registry so
  later office and clergy work can follow current and adult successors
  without reopening the office schema

## Explicit `v0.3` non-goals

The accepted office/service baseline intentionally does **not** include:

- autonomous office staffing gameplay
- clergy placement mechanics or institution-holder aging flows
- agenda item generation from offices
- UI surfaces that render raw office or service registries directly
- integrator-owned phase wiring for office succession or clergy persistence

Those remain later backlog scope, not missing pieces inside this epic.

## Handoff to agenda, family, and clergy work

Follow-on work may now treat the following as locked inputs:

- office seats and active service records have canonical ordering
- unchanged placement mirrors are tenure-stable
- holder replacement emits predecessor/successor continuity links
- institution-backed placements have an explicit assignment field
- office-holder succession hooks can point later features at the current
  heir and adult successor without recomputing claimant order

Follow-on work should not assume the following already exist:

- agenda items sourced from office pressure or vacancies
- clergy-track persistence across turns
- institution-holder registries beyond the bounded assignment seam
- UI or bounded snapshot surfaces that already expose raw office history

That leaves later agenda, family, and clergy work with a stable office and
service contract while preserving explicit room for integrator-owned phase
behavior and future people-placement systems.
