# LoM v0.3 Scope Lock

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-24
**Locked Decisions:** YES (CEO Lock)

This document is the authoritative, human-readable `v0.3.0` to `v0.3.4` scope lock used by `ops/v0.3/*`.

## Arc shape
- v0.3 has five sub-releases: `v0.3.0` through `v0.3.4`.
- Multi-manor portfolio baseline lands in `v0.3.2`.
- Map topology contract lands no later than `v0.3.1`.

## Cross-cutting principles
- Determinism contract: seeded RNG only, stable ordering, no `Math.random()`.
- Repo-as-memory: `ops/v0.3/*` controls unattended execution.
- Phase pipeline plus domain bounded contexts: phases own ordering, domains own meaning.
- Centralized write APIs: relationships via relationship engine, coin via ledger and receipts.
- Golden drift policy: any unexpected golden diff is an escalation.

## Category locks
### Simulation Core & Reliability
- `v0.3.0`: econ determinism harness, fiscal regression seed pack, golden receipt snapshots, perf guards. Lock Determinism & Receipts v1.
- `v0.3.1`: versioned state schema and migration scaffold for economy and portfolio registries.
- `v0.3.2`: multi-manor regression suite and invariants.
- `v0.3.3`: reliability quota and confirmed replay harness command.
- `v0.3.4`: hardening, hotspot simplification, docs refresh.

Budgets:
- snapshot cap: 204800 bytes per turn snapshot
- turn-time soft ceiling: 250 ms per seed on a dev machine

### People, Kinship & Dynasty Graph
- `v0.3.0`: marriage v1 bounded in one context with inbound and outbound offers, concurrent household offers, one pending offer per candidate, and 3-turn reject stickiness.
- `v0.3.1`: claims and inheritance baseline with succession-line map and bounded `inheritance_claim` prospects.
- `v0.3.2`: offices and service records groundwork, with seat filling capped at a one-turn vacancy.
- `v0.3.3`: clergy placements baseline with minimal institution holder support.
- `v0.3.4`: widowhood, remarriage, multiple-heir, orphaned-edge, and spouse-exclusivity hardening.

Map tie-in:
- persons must be assignable to `manor_id` for HoH, spouse, heirs, office holders, and anyone with active prospects.

### Economy & Fiscal Spine
- `v0.3.0`: ledger becomes canonical for coin and stores receipts, with deterministic rounding.
- `v0.3.1`: taxes, obligations, and enforcement with bounded penalties and inactive war hooks.
- `v0.3.2`: multi-manor portfolio baseline with rollups and outliers.
- `v0.3.3`: minimal pricing via fixed constants.
- `v0.3.4`: economy knobs and balance hooks.

Realism locks:
- Western Europe medieval blend.
- Settlement once per 3-year turn per counterparty.
- Assets tracked in `v0.3`: `coin`, `food_stores`, `meat_stores`.
- Gifts and offerings to liege and church are primary relationship levers.
- Persistent fiscal failure may end in dispossession game-over.

### CourtOS, Roles & Pacing
- `v0.3.0`: pacing scaffold v0 with decision budget of 6 per 3-year turn.
- `v0.3.1`: role slots and optional offices, with non-family retainer upkeep.
- `v0.3.2`: minimal delegation mechanics using deterministic efficiencies only.
- `v0.3.3`: pre-agenda scaffolding capturing what mattered.
