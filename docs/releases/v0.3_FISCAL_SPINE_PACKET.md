# LoM v0.3 Fiscal Spine Packet

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-26
**Owner:** CEO-in-the-loop

This document is the authoritative v0.3 fiscal realism contract backing Fiscal Ledger v1, Portfolio Accounting v1, and multi-manor expansion.

## Baseline
- Historical vibe: Western Europe medieval blend, not country-specific law simulation.
- Settlement cadence: once per 3-year turn per counterparty.
- Assets tracked in `v0.3`:
  - `coin`
  - `food_stores`
  - `meat_stores`
- In-kind service obligations remain a schema hook only.

Current `v0.3.0` baseline semantics:
- `coin` remains the canonical liquid settlement asset.
- `food_stores` currently aliases the legacy grain-store runtime balance backed by `manor.bushels_stored`.
- `meat_stores` is a distinct economy-owned balance and receipt surface used by hunting, consumption, and settlement helpers even though broader state-schema promotion remains a later step.

## Primitives
Income:
- rents and customary dues
- demesne surplus
- enterprises and rights
- justice and fee scaffold

Expenses:
- household and administration
- manor maintenance
- projects and construction

External counterparties:
- church
- liege or crown

Deferred counterparties:
- creditors
- guilds
- full market simulation
- active war loop

## Production and consumption baseline
Production sources:
- crop output remains the deterministic grain baseline
- hunting is a deterministic meat source derived from idle workforce, three-year turn cadence, and the player head's martial score

Consumption baseline:
- peasant and court grain demand preserve the legacy bushel baseline
- meat demand is an explicit bounded supplement with deterministic fallback substitution back to grain when reserves are short
- fixed-seed equilibrium fixtures cover balanced, hunting-reserve, and scarcity turns for regression review

## Obligations and enforcement
Obligation types:
- `church_due`
- `liege_due`
- `extraordinary_levy`

Arrears model:
- maintain `arrears_registry` per counterparty
- no negative coin; unpaid amounts roll into arrears

Penalty palette:
- relationship deltas via `applyRelationshipDelta`
- bounded unrest nudges
- placeholder legitimacy or piety effects if present

Tangible bite:
- seize enterprise income, capped per turn
- forced payment from stores, capped per turn

Failure ladder:
- stage 1: bounded penalties
- stage 2: tangible bite
- stage 3: dispossession or loss-of-seat game-over

## Ledger semantics
Every economically meaningful delta must resolve to an ordered receipt line.

Recommended receipt fields:
- `receipt_id`
- `turn`
- `category`
- `counterparty`
- `asset`
- `delta`
- `summary`
- `rule_id`
- `related_actor_ids`

Categories:
- `income.rents_dues`
- `income.demesne_surplus`
- `income.enterprises_rights`
- `income.justice_fees`
- `expense.household_admin`
- `expense.maintenance`
- `expense.project_capex`
- `obligation.church_settlement`
- `obligation.liege_settlement`
- `obligation.arrears_carry`
- `enforcement.penalty`
- `enforcement.seizure`
- `enforcement.forced_payment_stores`
- `gift.liege`
- `offering.church`

Current settlement scaffolds:
- accepted payment modes are explicit for `church_due`, `liege_due`, `extraordinary_levy`, `church_offering`, and `liege_gift`
- settlement helpers snapshot runtime asset paths for `coin`, `food_stores`, and `meat_stores`, plus a `service_placeholder` hook
- food and meat store debits should originate from economy-owned receipt writers so the mutation and receipt row remain coupled

## Multi-manor portfolio
- portfolio totals must equal the sum of manor totals for tracked assets and categories
- required outputs:
  - roll-up totals by category
  - per-manor net and outliers list

Expansion sources:
- inheritance and claims
- marriage outcomes
- liege grants through prospects

Relationship-driven grants:
- strongly depend on seeded liege relationship score
- world initialization must keep non-uniform relationships

Portfolio capacity:
- maintenance scaling
- admin overhead scaling with manor count
- delegation scaffolds with deterministic efficiencies

## Equilibrium and DOE
Track across seed packs:
- net coin per turn distribution
- arrears incidence and stage distribution
- dispossession rate
- manor count distribution over time
- gifts and offerings frequency with relationship impact
- food and meat store trajectories with shortage warnings

Acceptance intent:
- fiscal spine supports stable play with meaningful tradeoffs and central knob tuning
