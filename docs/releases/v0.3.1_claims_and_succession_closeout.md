## Scope

This closeout records the accepted `v0.3.1` claims and succession baseline
after `T01` through `T05`. It replaces the opening audit as the current
release-facing summary of what the people-domain succession seam now
guarantees, which claimant surfaces are intentionally bounded, and which
follow-on legal or grant systems are still out of scope.

## Accepted task sequence

- `T01` audited the live heir, claimant, and succession-order surfaces and
  documented the gap between imperative phase logic and the intended
  reusable people-domain seam.
- `T02` defined the canonical `succession_line_v0` and
  `claimant_registry_v0` schemas with deterministic direct-descendant and
  collateral ordering.
- `T03` routed bounded `inheritance_claim` prospect generation through that
  registry seam without adding court-trial or ledger mechanics.
- `T04` added deterministic cadet-branch and marriage-link relevance so
  claimant ordering can surface marriage-connected external branches after
  the canonical heir line is exhausted.
- `T05` exposed bounded succession-line and claimant summaries directly on
  preview-state and bounded snapshot surfaces so view-model consumers do
  not need to reconstruct heir logic from raw kinship data.

## Locked `v0.3.1` succession rules

### Ordered succession lines now exist as a reusable seam

The accepted baseline now guarantees:

- deterministic next-in-line entries for direct descendants
- deterministic male-line collateral fallback ordering
- stable line positions and overflow counts for bounded consumers
- explicit line metadata for house context and relevance reasons

This seam remains read-oriented. It does not replace the integrator-owned
runtime succession resolver yet, but it does give later work a stable
domain contract instead of phase-local heuristics.

### Claimants are bounded and claimant-aware

The accepted baseline now guarantees:

- bounded claimant registries keyed to the player house
- explicit current-heir and adult-successor mirrors
- claimant identity and target-line preview metadata on
  `inheritance_claim` prospects
- deterministic overflow counts when the full line is larger than the
  allowed preview window

The prospect path stays intentionally lightweight: it signals that a claim
exists and who currently fronts it, without inventing a separate trial,
petition, or adjudication subsystem.

### Marriage links and cadet branches now affect claimant relevance

The accepted relevance layer now guarantees:

- married-out descendant branches remain part of the ordered line
- cadet blood branches can surface as bounded claimants when the canonical
  heir line is empty
- marriage-linked external branches are ranked deterministically after
  blood cadets rather than being invisible or reconstructed ad hoc
- claimant ordering remains graph-stable even when the same external house
  is reachable both by descent and by marriage relevance

This is the key `v0.3.1` bridge between the marriage baseline and later
claims work: the repo now has a deterministic answer to “who matters next”
even when the ordinary heir pointer is empty.

### View-model and snapshot surfaces now carry the bounded summaries directly

The accepted output baseline now guarantees:

- `preview_state.succession_line_summary`
- `preview_state.claimant_summary`
- mirrored `house` copies of those summaries for existing household-facing
  consumers
- bounded snapshot serialization of the same summaries for turn-log and
  report surfaces

That means later UI work can read a stable top-N line plus overflow summary
directly, instead of reconstructing succession or claimant logic from raw
kinship edges in the presentation layer.

## Explicit `v0.3.1` non-goals

The accepted claims/succession baseline intentionally does **not** include:

- court-trial, adjudication, or appeal mechanics for claims
- ledger costs, relationship penalties, or obligation consequences for
  accepting or rejecting inheritance claims
- integrator-owned replacement of `phase_succession.ts` heir resolution
- non-player-house succession simulation beyond bounded summary seams
- grant, acquisition, clergy, or office transfer mechanics driven directly
  from claimant outcomes

Those remain later backlog scope, not missing pieces inside this epic.

## Handoff to grant, acquisition, and legal work

Follow-on work may now treat the following as locked inputs:

- succession lines and claimant registries have deterministic bounded order
- claimants can carry cadet and marriage-link relevance without bespoke
  graph walks
- `inheritance_claim` prospects already expose the claimant and target-line
  packet needed for lightweight UI and policy decisions
- preview-state and bounded snapshots already publish top-N succession and
  claimant summaries with overflow counts

Follow-on work should not assume the following already exist:

- full claim adjudication or dispute resolution
- automatic title transfer from claimant registry state
- multi-house legal persistence for external succession contests
- phase-level adoption of the new line ordering as the canonical heir
  runtime

That leaves later grant, acquisition, clergy, and legal work with a stable
succession input contract while preserving explicit room for integrator
phase adoption and deeper title-law systems.
