# v0.3.1 Residence-Manor Assignment Audit

Task: `V03-R1-006-T01`  
Date: `2026-04-09`  
Lane: `codex/v0.3-lane-social-mechanics`

## Scope

This audit inventories the current surfaces that imply where a person lives in
the world before the `V03-R1-006` baseline adds a deterministic
`residence_manor_id` contract.

## Current residence surfaces

- `src/sim/types.ts` only carries `residence_house_id` and `house_id`.
- `src/sim/worldgen.ts` seeds `residence_house_id` for the player head,
  spouse, children, and external houses, but no manor binding is attached to
  people.
- `src/sim/peopleFirst.ts` rehydrates missing `residence_house_id` values back
  to a house id, but it does not assign any manor id.
- `src/sim/domains/world/xmap.ts` exposes the canonical anchor manor and the
  numeric travel-distance hooks the lane needs, but no people-domain binding
  currently consumes them.

## Relevant people classes currently lacking manor bindings

- player-house head, spouse, children, and other members living at `h_player`
- local liege, clergy, and steward records that currently also live at
  `h_player`
- marriage-offer candidates and other external-house residents held in the
  people registry
- clergy placements and institution holders tracked by the new
  people-domain registries
- office and service holders derived from `service_records[]` and
  `house.court_office_registry`

## Existing transition points

- player-house world init in `src/sim/worldgen.ts`
- People-First rehydration in `src/sim/peopleFirst.ts`
- marriage acceptance paths in both `src/sim/phases/phase_prospects.ts` and
  `src/sim/domains/people/marriage.ts`
- clergy and institution-holder assignment in
  `src/sim/domains/people/clergyTrackRegistry.ts` and
  `src/sim/domains/people/institutionHolderRegistry.ts`
- office and service continuity through `service_records[]` and
  `house.court_office_registry`

## Gaps that block manor-aware higher-level surfaces today

- there is no canonical people-domain resolver from person to manor
- there is no snapshot-safe residence selector or numeric-distance hook surface
- external-house prospect people are real registry persons, but they do not
  currently map to any manor id
- office and clergy systems can say who serves where, but they cannot project
  a stable manor binding for those people

## Required transition points for the baseline

- a people-domain residence-manor resolver that treats the bounded world view
  anchor manor as the player-local manor seat
- deterministic rules for player-household residents, local clergy, and
  player-side service holders
- explicit unmapped selectors for external-house residents until house-to-manor
  world bindings exist
- snapshot-safe exposure of manor selectors and numeric-distance hooks without
  perturbing replay serialization
