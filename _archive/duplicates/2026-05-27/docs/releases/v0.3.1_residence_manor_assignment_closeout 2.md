## Scope

This closeout records the accepted `v0.3.1` residence-manor assignment
baseline after `T01` through `T04`. It supersedes the opening audit as the
release-facing summary of what manor-binding seams now guarantee, which
selectors and distance hooks are intentionally locked, and what later family,
portfolio, clergy, and UI work may safely assume.

## Accepted task sequence

- `T01` audited the current residence surfaces, missing manor bindings, and
  the transition points that would need a people-domain resolver.
- `T02` defined the canonical selector summary and numeric-distance hook rules
  for household, clergy, office, service, and external-house residents.
- `T03` added deterministic player-local manor binding plus coherent
  transition handling for marriage, clergy, institution-holder, and office or
  service-derived residency changes.
- `T04` exposed residence selectors, manor ids, and travel-distance hooks on
  preview-state and bounded-snapshot surfaces without perturbing replay
  serialization.
- `T05` locks the accepted invariants, publishes this closeout, and closes
  the epic for downstream portfolio and family UX work.

## Locked `v0.3.1` residence-manor rules

### Player-local residents bind to the world anchor manor

The accepted baseline now assumes:

- the bounded world topology anchor manor is the canonical player-local manor
- player-house head, spouse, children, and other player-side residents bind to
  that anchor manor
- local parish clergy and player-side service or office holders also resolve
  to that anchor manor when their active placement points at the player house
  or local parish

### External houses remain explicitly unmapped

The accepted baseline intentionally does not invent house-to-manor bindings
for external houses yet. External-house residents therefore expose:

- `residence_manor_id: null`
- `source_kind: external_house_unmapped`

That makes the missing mapping explicit and replay-safe while preserving room
for later world-level house-to-manor work.

### Residence selectors and distance hooks are canonical but hidden

The accepted selector surface now includes:

- `residence_selector_summary_v0`
- `residence_distance_hooks_v0`
- per-person hidden fields for `residence_manor_id`,
  `residence_selector_contexts`, and `residence_distance_hook`

These surfaces are attached to live state, preview-state, and bounded
snapshots as non-enumerable properties. Later consumers can read them
directly, but JSON serialization and replay hashes remain stable.

### Transition updates are deterministic

The accepted baseline now assumes:

- marriage acceptance can move a person onto the player-side manor binding
- clergy and institution-holder assignment refresh manor bindings coherently
- office and active service placement can project a player-local manor binding
- selector order remains canonical by person id

## Explicit `v0.3` non-goals

This residence-manor baseline intentionally does **not** include:

- canonical external house-to-manor world bindings
- integrator-owned phase or turn-orchestration rewiring
- UI panels that directly render manor-distance tables
- new replay-visible snapshot keys for manor bindings
- automatic travel or routing gameplay that consumes the distance hooks

Those remain later backlog scope, not missing pieces in this epic.

## Handoff

Follow-on work may now treat the following as locked inputs:

- player-local residents resolve to the bounded world anchor manor
- clergy, institution-holder, office, and service transitions refresh manor
  bindings deterministically
- preview-state and bounded snapshots expose hidden manor selector and
  distance-hook surfaces
- external-house residents remain explicitly unmapped until world bindings
  exist

Follow-on work should not assume the following already exist:

- world-authored manor ids for every non-player house
- replay-visible manor selector fields on the serialized snapshot body
- live UI rendering of raw residence selector registries
