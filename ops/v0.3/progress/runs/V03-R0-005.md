## Accepted diff notes

- `v0.3.0` world init now satisfies the scope-lock requirement for
  non-uniform seeded relationship edges and scores at turn zero.
- Known-world relevance is deterministic and bounded, and direct kinship or
  marriage ties can now elevate houses into the surfaced social set.
- Experience layers no longer need to infer hidden social state for the
  known-world baseline because preview and snapshot surfaces already expose
  coarse canonical summaries.
- The accepted baseline still intentionally stops short of deep local-house
  dynasty graphs, unified church-holder modeling, and grant or claims logic
  that consumes seeded relationship strength directly.
