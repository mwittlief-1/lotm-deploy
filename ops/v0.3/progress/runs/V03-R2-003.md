## Accepted diff notes

- Delegation is now a deterministic efficiency layer with fixed action
  ordering and no hidden variance.
- Gift and offering delegation preserves the canonical fiscal settlement and
  receipt paths.
- Marriage scouting can delegate budget cost, but not below one court
  decision, and without changing candidate or RNG behavior.
- Bounded snapshots now expose `court_delegation_view`, so downstream court
  and portfolio readers can consume delegation state without recomputing it
  from raw registry internals.
- The epic intentionally stops short of autonomous agenda logic, ordinary
  obligation delegation, and office/service activation.
