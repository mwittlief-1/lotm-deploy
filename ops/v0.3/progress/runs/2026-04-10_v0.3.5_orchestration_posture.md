## Operating Rules

- Treat `R4` carry-over as a hard unlock, not a soft assumption.
- Keep one active claimed task per lane, but shape backlog chains so a lane can continue locally after acceptance without waiting for a fresh operator prompt.
- Pre-promote the immediate same-lane successor when its only unmet dependency is the in-flight predecessor and no checkpoint boundary applies.
- Reserve kickoff-only integration for:
  - `src/sim/turn.ts`
  - cross-phase sequencing
  - cross-lane contract stitching
  - control-plane mutations
- Prefer explicit bounded contracts over ad hoc UI data reads or direct state mutation.
